Repost Companion Privacy Policy

Repost Companion is a manual, local-only campaign tracker. It does not read or modify RepostExchange or SoundCloud pages.

Information stored

When you use the extension, it stores track titles, artist names, listening notes, comment drafts, and the completion times you enter. This information is kept in Chrome's storage.local on your device so the extension can show your progress and avoid repeating recent drafts.

Information sharing

The extension does not send this information to the developer or any other server. It contains no analytics, advertising, remote code, account service, or AI connection. The copy button writes a draft to your clipboard only when you select it.

Permissions

  • storage saves your log and drafts locally in Chrome.
  • clipboardWrite copies a draft after you select the copy button.

Repost Companion requests no website access or host permissions.

Data removal

You can remove individual log entries using the extension. Removing the extension from Chrome removes its local extension data.

Changes and contact

This policy applies to Repost Companion version 1.0.0. A publisher support contact must be added here before public distribution.
